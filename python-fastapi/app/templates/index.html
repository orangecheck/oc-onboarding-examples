A site with its own login, and orangecheck behind it.

This is a reference: fork it, run it, and read how each step works. It walks the whole funnel a person meets.

  1. Sign up with this site’s own login. An email and a password, kept here.
  2. Get an orangecheck account through Connect. Without asking, or with an opt-in box: you choose which.
  3. Claim it at ochk.app. Then it is the person’s, to use on other sites or delete.
  4. Sign in with orangecheck. On its own, or linked to the account they already have here.
  5. Move to their own keys. Not open yet: orangecheck holds every key that can move funds while the system is in testing. See ochk.app/custody.
